Test the Platform Yourself

I don't show earnings or withdrawal proofs here because those should come from your own experience. Before making any decision, check the following:

  • What is the platform's business model? Where do the earnings actually come from?
  • What are the minimum withdrawal amount, payout methods, and fees?
  • Does it ask for a deposit or a paid upgrade? If so, be very cautious.
  • Do earnings depend on inviting others more than on using the service?
  • What do users say on independent sites and forums outside the platform?

Points to Evaluate

May be worthwhile if

  • Trying it is free and requires no deposit.
  • Withdrawal terms are clear and publicly stated.
  • You actually manage to withdraw a small amount.

Red flags

  • Promises of fast or guaranteed profits.
  • Asking for money before your first withdrawal.
  • Unexplained difficulty or delays when withdrawing.
  • No clear information about the company or support.

How to Start Carefully

1. Read the terms

Fees, minimum withdrawal, and account policies.

2. Start without a deposit

Don't pay before you're sure the service works.

3. Withdraw a small amount

Don't trust the platform until your first payout reaches you.
